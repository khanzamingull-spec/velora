Privacy Policy

Information about how this website handles customer information.

This page describes the current demonstration version of Velora. Privacy practices should be reviewed and updated before a real public launch.

1. Information We Collect

When you place an order or send a message, the website may collect information such as your name, phone number, email address, city, delivery address and information you choose to provide.

2. How Information Is Used

Information may be used to process orders, respond to customer questions and provide information related to store activity.

3. Local Storage

The current demonstration website uses browser local storage for shopping cart information, orders and contact messages. This is temporary front-end storage and should not be treated as secure production data storage.

4. Information Security

A production version should use appropriate server-side security, authentication, database protection and secure data handling.

5. Third-Party Services

The current demonstration version does not rely on a connected payment processor or production customer database. Any future third-party services should be reviewed before being added.

6. Policy Updates

This policy may be updated when the Velora website, services or data practices change.
