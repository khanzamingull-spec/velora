VELORA • DELIVERY POLICY

Shipping & Returns

Information about order processing, delivery, cancellations and returns.

```
```
Demo Store Notice: Velora is currently being developed as a demonstration ecommerce website. Actual delivery times, shipping charges and return conditions should be confirmed and updated before the store launches for real customers.

1. Order Processing

Orders are currently recorded through the demonstration checkout system. In a real store, orders should be reviewed and confirmed before being prepared for delivery.

2. Delivery

Delivery time may depend on the customer's city, area, product availability and delivery service. Customers should receive an estimated delivery timeframe when their order is confirmed.

3. Shipping Charges

Shipping charges may vary depending on location, package size, order value and the delivery service used. Actual charges should be displayed clearly before an order is confirmed.

4. Cash on Delivery

The current demonstration checkout supports Cash on Delivery. If this payment method is offered after launch, customers should be informed of the payment process and any applicable order conditions before delivery.

5. Order Cancellations

Customers may request cancellation before an order has been dispatched, subject to the actual store policy. Once an order has been shipped, cancellation may no longer be possible.

6. Returns

Return eligibility should depend on the final return policy established by the store. Before launch, the policy should clearly explain the return period, eligible products, product condition requirements and the return process.

7. Damaged or Incorrect Items

If a customer receives an item that appears damaged or differs from the confirmed order, the customer should contact Velora as soon as possible with the relevant order information.

8. Product Availability

Product availability may change. If an ordered product becomes unavailable, the customer should be contacted and informed about the available options.

9. Delivery Information

Customers should provide a complete and accurate delivery address and contact number. Incorrect or incomplete information may result in delivery delays or an unsuccessful delivery.

10. Questions About an Order

For questions about an order, delivery or returns, customers should contact Velora using the contact information provided on the website.

Visit the Velora contact page to send a message.
